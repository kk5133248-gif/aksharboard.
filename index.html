Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

The short version

What you type in AksharBoard stays on your phone. Translation from English to Hindi happens on your device, not on a server, and we never upload, store or read your messages.

We don't have accounts, we don't show ads, and we don't sell data. The only data that leaves your phone is technical diagnostic information sent to Google by the translation library we use, and payment details handled by Google Play.

This policy explains how the AksharBoard keyboard app for Android (“AksharBoard”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. AksharBoard is developed by Mithun Kumar, Bhagalpur Bihar, India.

What happens to what you type

When you use AksharBoard as your keyboard, it receives the keys you press, like every keyboard on Android does. Android shows a standard warning about this when you enable any keyboard.

  • In the ABC tab, each key you press is passed directly to the app you are typing in. AksharBoard does not save it.
  • In the Hindi translation tab, the English text you type is held in the phone's memory only while you are writing, so it can be translated. It is cleared when you tap Send or Clear, and it is never written to storage.
  • Translation between the languages you choose is done on your phone by Google ML Kit's on-device translation. Your text is not sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else to be translated.
  • AksharBoard reads your clipboard only at the moment you tap its Paste button, to paste the copied text into your app or into the translation box. It never checks your clipboard in the background and never stores what you paste. Text that another app marks as a copied password is not placed in the translation box.
  • If you tap Copy, the translation is placed on your phone's clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. Nothing else is saved.
  • When you are typing in a password field, AksharBoard switches back to the normal tab automatically, so passwords never enter the translation box.
  • We do not collect, log, upload or share your typing, messages, words or translations.

Voice typing

If you tap the microphone button, AksharBoard asks for microphone permission and uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (on most Android phones, provided by Google) to turn your speech into text. The microphone is used only while the button shows that it is listening. AksharBoard does not record, store or upload your voice itself. Depending on your phone and language, the speech service may process your voice on the device or send it to its own servers; that processing is covered by the speech service provider's privacy policy, such as the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your phone's settings.

Downloading language packs

To translate offline, the app downloads a language model (about 30 MB each) from Google's servers once for every language you choose, starting with English and Hindi. This requires an internet connection. Like any download, Google's servers receive standard technical information such as your IP address to deliver the file. After the download, translation works without internet.

Diagnostic data collected by Google ML Kit

AksharBoard uses Google ML Kit for translation. According to Google, the ML Kit library collects device information (such as phone model and Android version), app information, performance metrics and similar technical data for diagnostics and usage analytics. It also uses Firebase installation identifiers for this purpose. This data does not include what you type.

This data goes directly to Google, is encrypted in transit, and is handled under Google's own policies. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. You can read Google's details in the ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Payments and AksharBoard Pro

AksharBoard Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment, and we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns AksharBoard Pro, so it can unlock unlimited translations and restore Pro if you reinstall. Google handles your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

Information stored on your phone

To run the free trial and daily limit, the app saves a few small settings on your phone:

  • the date you first used the keyboard (for the 7-day trial),
  • how many free translations you have used today,
  • whether AksharBoard Pro is unlocked.

These settings are not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include them in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use our own analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your personal information.
  • We don't access your contacts, location, photos or files, and the microphone is used only when you tap it for voice typing.

Children

AksharBoard is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can stop using AksharBoard at any time by switching to another keyboard in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Settings → Apps → AksharBoard, deletes all settings and the language pack from your phone.
  • Because we don't keep your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For data held by Google, such as purchases and diagnostics, you can use your Google account's privacy controls.

Security

Keeping your typing on your phone is our main security measure: data we never collect can't be leaked. Connections to Google for the language pack, diagnostics and payments are encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If we change how AksharBoard handles information, for example by adding a new feature, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes on Google Play.

Contact

For questions, requests or complaints about privacy, write to aksharboard.support@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 days.
